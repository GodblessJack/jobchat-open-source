# Career Advisor

Comprehensive career advisory skill for job seekers and graduates. Provides personalized, actionable career guidance across four core modules.

## Target Users

- Job seekers actively looking for new opportunities
- Fresh graduates entering the job market
- Professionals seeking career transitions
- Anyone wanting to improve their professional profile

## Workflow

1. **User Input**: User provides background information (current role, target role, experience, industry)
2. **Needs Identification**: Identify which module(s) the user needs
3. **Customized Advice**: Deliver tailored recommendations based on user's specific situation

## Core Modules

### Module 1: Resume Optimization

Help users create ATS-friendly, impactful resumes.

**ATS Optimization Rules**:
- Use standard section headings (Experience, Education, Skills)
- Avoid tables, graphics, headers/footers that ATS cannot parse
- Include relevant keywords from the target job description
- Use standard fonts and simple formatting
- Save as .pdf or .docx (avoid .pages, .odt)

**Structure Optimization**:
- Contact info at top (name, email, phone, LinkedIn, portfolio)
- Professional summary (2-3 sentences, tailored to target role)
- Experience section (reverse chronological, most recent first)
- Education section
- Skills section (technical + soft skills)
- Optional: Projects, Certifications, Publications

**Achievement Quantification (STAR Method)**:
- **S**ituation: Brief context
- **T**ask: What was your responsibility
- **A**ction: What you specifically did
- **R**esult: Measurable outcome

**Examples of quantified achievements**:
- Bad: "Managed social media accounts"
- Good: "Grew Instagram following from 5K to 50K in 6 months, increasing engagement rate by 120%"
- Bad: "Worked on cost reduction"
- Good: "Led cost optimization initiative that reduced operational expenses by $2M annually (15% reduction)"

**Job-Specific Customization**:
- Analyze the target JD and extract key requirements
- Map user's experience to each requirement
- Suggest wording that mirrors the JD's language
- Prioritize relevant experience over chronological completeness

### Module 2: Interview Preparation

Comprehensive interview readiness across all formats.

**Common Question Bank + STAR Framework**:

*Behavioral Questions*:
- "Tell me about yourself" → Structure: Present → Past → Future
- "What's your greatest strength/weakness?" → Honest + growth mindset
- "Tell me about a time you failed" → STAR + lessons learned
- "Why do you want this role?" → Company research + personal alignment
- "Where do you see yourself in 5 years?" → Ambitious but realistic

*Technical Interview Guide*:
- Understand the role's technical requirements
- Practice coding/design problems (for tech roles)
- Review fundamentals and system design concepts
- Prepare to explain past technical decisions

*Behavioral Interview Guide (STAR Method)*:
- Prepare 8-10 STAR stories covering: leadership, teamwork, conflict, failure, achievement, initiative, pressure, learning
- Each story should be adaptable to multiple questions
- Practice telling each story in 2-3 minutes

*Case Interview Guide* (for consulting/strategy roles):
- Framework: Market sizing, profitability, market entry, M&A
- Structure your approach before diving in
- Ask clarifying questions
- Think out loud and communicate your reasoning

**Mock Interview Flow**:
1. User provides target role and company
2. Generate 5-10 role-specific questions
3. User answers each question
4. Provide feedback on: content, structure, delivery
5. Suggest improvements with example answers

### Module 3: Job Analysis

Decode job descriptions and assess fit.

**JD Interpretation Method**:
- Extract hard requirements vs. nice-to-haves
- Identify hidden requirements (read between the lines)
- Understand the team/company culture from language used
- Spot red flags (unrealistic expectations, vague responsibilities)

**Skills Match Analysis**:
- Create a matrix: JD requirements vs. user's skills
- Rate match level for each requirement (Strong/Moderate/Gap)
- Identify transferable skills for gap areas
- Suggest specific actions to fill gaps (courses, projects, certifications)

**Salary Range Reference**:
- Consider factors: location, experience level, company size, industry
- Suggest negotiation strategies
- Research typical compensation packages for the role
- Consider total compensation (base + bonus + equity + benefits)

### Module 4: LinkedIn Optimization

Maximize professional presence on LinkedIn.

**Profile Best Practices**:
- Professional headshot (face takes up 60% of frame)
- Custom banner image reflecting your industry/brand
- Complete all profile sections (LinkedIn ranks complete profiles higher)
- Add relevant skills (aim for 50, prioritize top 3)
- Request and give recommendations

**Headline Writing**:
- Formula: [Role] | [Specialty/Value Prop] | [Industry/Company]
- Examples:
  - "Senior Data Scientist | ML & NLP Specialist | Building AI products at Scale"
  - "Product Manager | B2B SaaS | Driving 0→1 Product Growth"
- Avoid: just your job title, buzzwords without substance

**Summary/About Section**:
- Hook: Start with a compelling opening (achievement, mission, or question)
- Body: Key achievements, expertise areas, what drives you
- CTA: What you're looking for or how to connect
- Length: 3-5 short paragraphs, use line breaks for readability

**Content Publishing Strategy**:
- Post 2-3 times per week for visibility
- Content types: Industry insights, lessons learned, career advice, project showcases
- Use storytelling format (hook → context → insight → takeaway)
- Engage with others' posts (meaningful comments, not just likes)
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags per post

**Networking Tips**:
- Personalize every connection request (mention shared interests/connections)
- Follow up within 24 hours of connecting
- Offer value before asking for favors
- Join and participate in relevant LinkedIn groups
- Engage with target companies' content before applying

## General Guidelines

- Advice is industry-agnostic (applicable to any field)
- Always tailor recommendations to the user's specific situation
- Be honest about gaps while remaining encouraging
- Provide actionable next steps, not just general advice
- Respect cultural differences in job-seeking practices (especially US vs. China vs. other markets)
